This was a final project at the end of my DEC in CEGEP. 
It required that we use an arduino to create an IoT device. 
My partner and I then spent about 9 months creating the ROPOT. 
From breadboard diagrams, PCB design, 3D modeling, hand soldering, 
parts sourcing, programming, html website design, this thing really had it all. 
I love it very much.
