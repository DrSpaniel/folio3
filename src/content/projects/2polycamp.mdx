PolyCAMP is a game that was made during 2025’s edition of the scientific game jam. 
The concept comes from a PhD research paper on how polyamorous relationships function, as well as the different kinds of dynamics that pop up. 

We came up with a “love island” style game, where NPCs show up to find 1 or more people to interact with. 
Depending on their details, you decide where to put them for maximum compatibility. 

We won the “Tabous à nu” award at the ceremony as well!
